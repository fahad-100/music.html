ABOUT US                                    
 THE GUITAR GARAGE                   


The guitar garage.pak is a searchable database of pakistani guitar/bass, amp, effects,
and accessory builders.

Over the years I’ve spent many hours scouring forums, facebook groups, and old blog articles for pakistani gear companies.
I’m always looking to expand my gear collection but most days I search out of pure interest.
In an effort to make it easier for others making the same search and to help promote pakistani companies, I created this site.
If you have a suggestion for a company to add, you can submit here.
If you’re just looking to get in touch, you can reach THE GUITAR GARAGE here.






A little about me. I’m fahad and my interest in pakistani gear started after hearing my buddies Garnet amp.
I had to have my own, and I had to know what else was out there. This led me on many gear safaris, in person and online.
Since an early age I’ve been playing music and my everyday grind is a Graphic & Web Designer.
This site is a mix of my love for both interests. If you’re looking for design or web work, like maybe a new site to sell your pedals, amps, guitars?
You can reach me here.
